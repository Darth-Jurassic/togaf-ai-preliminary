# Preliminary 0001: Describe Product Context

- Record: Reusable policy
- Tags: preliminary, product-context, scope, ownership

## Context

Reusable process guidance must not silently supply an adopting organization's
product identity, business model, tenancy, ownership or deployment assumptions.

## Decision

An adopting project records its product or capability purpose, affected
stakeholders, organizational/tenant boundaries, baseline evidence, exclusions and
decision authority before defining target architecture. Canonical Location and
Motivation elements express those definitions; a repository name is not evidence
of ownership. Requirements remain explicit and traceable to the source signal.

## Consequences

Process and notation can be shared without copying a private architecture model.
Context refinements reopen only affected prerequisite gates. Product naming is
governed by [Preliminary 0014](0014-define-project-naming.md), and lean process
requirements by [Preliminary 0002](0002-align-architecture-work-with-lean-software-product-development.md).
